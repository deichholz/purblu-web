Jean Spencer is a business and technology leader, executive coach, and mentor with more than 30 years of experience. Her career began in neurology nursing and has included roles as chief technology officer, business unit president, head of engineering, chief financial officer, and chief operating officer. She also served as a senior managing director at Accenture.

After retiring from her corporate career, Jean focused on executive coaching, mentoring young adults, and ministry work, including work in India. She established Morning Glory Valley in Jordan and helped develop PurBlu as a business that could support the farm’s mission.
