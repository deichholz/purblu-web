Tim Gregory leads PurBlu’s daily operations, including monitoring growing systems, tracking production data, and raising shrimp. A Bloomington native, he first worked for Jean Spencer doing landscaping at Morning Glory Valley. He later became PurBlu’s first employee and helped design and build the shrimp-growing facility.

Tim’s experience includes restaurant work, ski instruction, and a pallet liquidation business he built with Jean. That business gave him experience with systems and spreadsheets that he now uses at PurBlu. His work with Jean began before the shrimp venture and continues through his role managing the farm’s daily operations.
