The idea began as Jean Spencer and Mike explored ways to make Morning Glory Valley financially sustainable. Mike’s research led them to shrimp farming, and that practical possibility grew into PurBlu.

Today, Jean’s experience in business leadership and mentoring works alongside Tim Gregory’s hands-on leadership of the farm. Tim helped design and build the growing facility and now manages daily operations—monitoring the system, tracking the farm’s data, and caring for the shrimp.

The goal is both immediate and long-term: raise excellent shrimp for local customers, build a business that lasts, and help sustain the broader work happening at the farm.
