PurBlu raises Pacific white shrimp indoors in Jordan, Minnesota, for local customers. The business was developed to help fund Morning Glory Valley’s mentoring and community work with young adults. Jean Spencer brings more than 30 years of business and technology leadership experience to the venture. Tim Gregory helped design and build the facility and leads daily operations.
