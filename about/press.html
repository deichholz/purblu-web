Company background

PurBlu grew from an effort to build a lasting farm business at Morning Glory Valley—a place centered on mentoring, hospitality, and community. The shrimp operation is intended to help sustain that broader work while offering a fresh local product.

Jean Spencer

Jean is a business and technology leader, executive coach, and mentor with more than 30 years of experience. Her career has included neurology nursing and senior roles across technology, engineering, finance, operations, and business leadership. In Jordan, she brings that experience to Morning Glory Valley and PurBlu.

Tim Gregory

Tim leads PurBlu’s day-to-day farm operations. A Bloomington native, he helped design and build the shrimp-growing facility. His varied background in restaurants, ski instruction, landscaping, and small business now supports the daily work of monitoring systems, tracking data, and raising shrimp.
