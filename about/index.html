Our story

Fresh shrimp—and a farm built for something bigger.

PurBlu is a local shrimp business at Morning Glory Valley in Jordan, Minnesota. It began with a practical question: how could the farm build a lasting source of income while creating room for mentoring, meaningful work, and community?
