Morning Glory Valley was established as a place where young adults could find discipleship, mentoring, encouragement, and opportunities to grow. Jean partnered with Mike and Deb to shape a farm community that brings faith, hospitality, and practical life experience together.

Over time, the way that mission is expressed has evolved. Mentoring continues, former residents and visitors return for meals and connection, and the farm remains a welcoming place where relationships can take root.

PurBlu is the first farm business growing from that vision. By pairing meaningful work with a sustainable local business, the shrimp operation adds a new chapter to Morning Glory Valley’s story.
