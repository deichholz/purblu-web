# Internal Documentation Rules

Everything in this directory is an internal development/review record and is excluded from the public build. Its presence does not constitute publication approval.

- `content-register.md` records verified inputs, customer-flow status, conflicts, and unresolved facts. Update it when a business fact or integration is confirmed, superseded, or found invalid.
- `assets.md` records asset provenance, rights/status, public use, captions, alt text, and replacement instructions.
- `module-copy.md`, `press-copy.md`, and `preparation-copy.md` contain draft or staged material. Move content into public source only after the relevant factual, editorial, rights, safety, and business approvals.
- Preserve source citations and note the date of time-sensitive checks. For food handling, use current authoritative public-health sources and re-check them before publication.
- Recipe drafts must be kitchen-tested with actual harvest-size shrimp before publishing yield, serving count, or timing. Keep clear that one pound whole yields less than one pound peeled meat.
- Press biographies/releases require final names, roles, dates, relationship wording, contacts, quotations, image permissions, and publication approval. Do not expose old PDFs/DOCX with conflicts or placeholders.
- Never turn an internal aspiration, cost, sales target, proposed email cadence, or inferred customer promise into public copy without approval.
- Record conflicts rather than silently choosing a convenient source. The current shared JSON governs rendered harvest/site values; documentation should explain discrepancies and their resolution.
- Do not place service secrets, customer records, real order data, or unapproved subscriber information here.
