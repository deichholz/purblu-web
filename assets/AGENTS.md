# Public Asset Rules

Top-level CSS, WebP, PNG, and ICO files in this directory are copied into the public build. `originals/` is excluded and preserves master material.

- Use approved real PurBlu shrimp, tank, harvest, sorting, packing, pickup, and people photography when available.
- Never use ocean trawlers, tropical ponds, imported-seafood imagery, or an image that implies a process PurBlu does not use.
- AI or illustrated placeholders must be clearly documented and must not be presented as documentary evidence of actual product, inventory, packaging, people, or facilities.
- Preserve original aspect ratios. Update source files, `srcset`, intrinsic width/height, alt text, and captions together when replacing an image.
- Record source, rights/permission, approval date, pictured identities, caption, and web derivatives in `docs/assets.md`.
- Keep master files in `assets/originals/`; do not publish that directory or bulk-copy it into `dist/`.
- Do not stretch, crop carelessly, recolor, redraw, or add effects to the logo. Use the approved Drive source and retain a readable digital width of at least 140 px.
- Optimize public raster assets for the web without destroying useful masters. Verify visual quality, file size, dimensions, transparency, and mobile cropping after replacement.
- Alt text describes the meaningful visible content; captions disclose illustration/serving-example status or other context that visitors need.
- `site.css` implements the brand system. Preserve accessible contrast, responsive layouts, visible focus, reduced accidental overflow, and comfortable touch targets.
